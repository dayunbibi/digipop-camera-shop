Shipping Policy

Everything you need to know about delivery, processing time, and package handling for DigiPop camera orders.

Processing Time

Orders are usually processed within 1–3 business days after payment is confirmed. Since DigiPop focuses on pre-owned digital cameras, each item is checked before shipping.

Estimated Delivery

Canada 3–7 business days
United States 5–10 business days
International 10–20 business days

Shipping Cost

Standard shipping is free on selected camera orders. Some international orders may require additional shipping fees depending on destination, package size, and carrier availability.

Package Safety

Cameras are packed carefully with protective material to reduce movement during delivery. Each package is prepared with extra care because digital cameras are delicate electronic items.

Tracking

Once your order ships, a tracking number will be sent by email. Tracking updates may take 24–48 hours to appear after the carrier receives the package.

Important Note

DigiPop is a demo portfolio shop. This page is designed to look like a real e-commerce shipping policy, but checkout and shipping are not active.
