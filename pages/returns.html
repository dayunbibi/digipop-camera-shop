Returns Policy

Learn how DigiPop handles returns, exchanges, item condition, and camera order concerns.

Return Window

Return requests should be made within 7 days of delivery. Since most cameras are pre-owned, all return requests are reviewed based on the item condition and order details.

Eligible Returns

  • The wrong camera was delivered.
  • The item arrived damaged during shipping.
  • The camera condition is significantly different from the product description.
  • The package is missing an included accessory listed on the product page.

Non-Returnable Cases

  • Change of mind after opening and using the camera.
  • Minor cosmetic wear already described on the product page.
  • Damage caused by dropping, water exposure, or incorrect charging.
  • Missing original packaging or included accessories after delivery.

How to Request a Return

1

Contact DigiPop through the contact page.

2

Include your order number, product name, and reason for return.

3

Attach clear photos if the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

4

Wait for return approval before sending the item back.

Refunds

Once the returned item is received and inspected, refunds are processed back to the original payment method. Processing time may vary depending on the bank or payment provider.

Important Note

DigiPop is a demo portfolio shop. This page is built to demonstrate a realistic online store return policy, but real returns are not active.
